Long Table Festival

One long table. Many family stories. A welcoming celebration of food, friendship, and the community we build together.

Come share the table.

The Long Table Festival is an easygoing gathering for CACC families, friends, and neighbors. Bring your appetite and your curiosity—we’ll share a meal, meet someone new, and enjoy activities for all ages.
