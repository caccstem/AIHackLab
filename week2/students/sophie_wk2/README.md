# Momo's Moonlit City

A cozy side-scrolling platform game. Guide Momo across the city rooftops, collect lanterns, and reach the tram home.

## Play

Open `index.html` with Live Server. Use **Left/Right** or **A/D** to move and **Space**, **Up**, or **W** to jump. Press **P** or **Escape** to pause.

No install or build step is needed.
