ANALYSIS OF SURVEY RESULTS
————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
    
    Based on the 15 survey responses, the results indicate that students primarily need assistance with organizing their academic responsibilities, managing their time, staying focused, and maintaining motivation.

    The most common difficulty was organizing assignments, notes, and deadlines, selected by 66.7% of respondents. This was followed by time management and scheduling at 60%. Additionally, 53.3% of respondents struggled with staying focused and avoiding distractions, while the same percentage experienced difficulty getting started when feeling overwhelmed.

    Regarding desired website features, flashcards and quizzes ranked first, receiving 80% of responses. To-do lists and task management followed at 73.3%, while calendar and deadline tracking and progress tracking each received 66.7%. AI-powered study assistance, customizable themes, and gamification were also popular, each receiving 53.3%.

    These findings suggest that students want a website that combines learning and productivity features in one accessible platform. Rather than focusing exclusively on scheduling or task management, the website should provide tools that actively support learning, organization, and motivation.

    Therefore, the proposed website should prioritize interactive learning features, academic organization tools, personalized progress tracking, and engaging motivational elements. The overall goal is to create a simple, convenient, and enjoyable platform that helps students become more productive while improving their studying habits.

MAIN PROJECT IDEA OVERVIEW
————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
    
    General Overview: A study and productivity website designed to help students organize their academic responsibilities, manage their time, study more effectively, and stay motivated. The platform combines productivity tools and interactive learning features into one accessible and engaging website.

    Target Audience: Middle and high school students who
        - struggle with time management
        - procrastinate and feel overwhelmed
        - want to improve their study habits
        - prefer interactive and engaging learning experiences

WEBSITE FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALITY
————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
    
    Dashboard: Display daily tasks, upcoming deadlines, progress, and study goals.
    To-do list: Allow users to add, edit, delete, and complete tasks.
    Calendar: Display assignments, exams, deadlines, and study sessions.
    Flashcards: Allow users to create flashcards and review them.
    Quizzes: Generate interactive quizzes based on study materials.
    AI study assistant: Help explain topics, generate study materials, and answer questions.
    Study planner: Generate personalized study schedules based on deadlines and available time.
    Pomodoro timer: Provide customizable focus and break sessions.
    Progress tracker: Track completed tasks, study time, goals, and achievements.
    Gamification: Award points, streaks, badges, and achievements.
    Notes: Allow users to create and organize digital notes.
    Customization: Allow users to personalize themes, colors, and layouts.
